Two rules worth repeating up front, because they are the usual tells of off-brand output:

- **No emoji, anywhere.** Use the 24-glyph icon set in `assets/icons/`.
- **Shadows are blue-tinted** (`rgba(16,6,158,…)`), never neutral grey or black.
